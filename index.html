How it works

Welcome to Text List

  1. Add your list by pasting phone numbers or uploading a CSV.
  2. Choose the phone, display-name, merge-field, and ignored columns.
  3. Write a message using fields like {{First Name}}.
  4. Review missing data and customize fallback messages when needed.
  5. Text on this device, or transfer the prepared list with QR codes.
Your contact list and message content stay in your browser. The app does not need a server to process them.
