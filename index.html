How it works

Welcome to Text-o-Matic

  1. Add your list by pasting phone numbers or uploading a CSV.
  2. Choose the phone column, the name shown for each recipient, merge fields, and ignored columns.
  3. Write a message using fields like {{First Name}}.
  4. Review missing data and customize fallback messages when needed.
  5. Text on this device, or transfer the prepared list with QR codes.
Your data stays private. Text-o-Matic processes your phone numbers, names, merge fields, and messages entirely in your browser. They are never uploaded to Text-o-Matic or sent to a server. If you use QR transfer, your prepared contact and message data is encoded directly into the QR code and moved between your devices without being uploaded to or exposed on the internet.
